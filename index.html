Flagship Research

ADAPTIVE

A Specialized Parallel Architecture for Resource-Constrained Pattern Recognition

ADAPTIVE investigates parallel pattern evaluation

ADAPTIVE investigates parallel pattern evaluation as an alternative to sequential evaluation for resource-constrained pattern-recognition workloads. The architecture evaluates multiple Hamming distances in parallel and then uses a logarithmic reduction structure to identify the best match.

      ↓
8 × HAMMING PARALLEL UNITS
      ↓
MIN / BEST MATCH

8 → 4 → 2 → 1 → MIN
